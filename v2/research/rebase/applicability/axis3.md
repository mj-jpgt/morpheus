# Axis A3 — NL⇄biology grounding + emergent-knowledge elicitation *and its evaluation*

**Remit.** A3 asks whether a multimodal tumor-state trunk actually *knows* biology (pathway/mechanism structure), whether that knowledge can be *elicited* through a natural-language interface, and — the load-bearing clause — how to *measure* it credibly. The literature makes one thing unambiguous: for A3, the novelty is the **measurement method and the grounding interface**, not a claim to emergence. Nearly every "emergent biology" claim in adjacent work is either a metric artifact, a text-prior in disguise, or a site confound.

Sources: primarily `l06_emergence_eval` (the core lane), with `l04_molecular_nl_prompting`, `l11_benchmarks_confound`, `l09_alignment_identifiability`, `l15_steelman_prior_art`, `l05_promptable_unified`.

## Key findings (with citations)

### 1. "Emergence" is contested and easy to fake; the contribution must be the metric, not the claim
- **Emergence is often a metric mirage.** Swapping exact-match/accuracy for a smooth per-token metric turns "sharp emergence" into continuous, predictable curves (25/29 metrics); too-small test sets manufacture the illusion (Schaeffer, Miranda, Koyejo, NeurIPS 2023, arXiv:2304.15004). **Implication:** any biological-knowledge "jump" MORPHEUS reports must be shown under *both* smooth and thresholded scoring with adequate test-set size, or a reviewer dismisses it.
- **Aggregate loss is predictable; specific capabilities are not** (Ganguli et al., Anthropic, FAccT 2022, arXiv:2202.07785). MORPHEUS cannot infer "the model knows biology" from trunk loss or C-index — capability-specific probes are mandatory. This is the direct empirical backing for the thesis claim that the current harness is "structurally blind to representation quality."
- **Emergence is mostly *elicitation*, not creation.** Purported emergent abilities decompose into in-context learning + memory + linguistic knowledge (Lu et al., ACL 2024, arXiv:2309.01809). **Implication:** reframe A3 as "can we *elicit* latent biology the trunk already encodes," not "the model spontaneously invented biology."
- **Index emergence by loss, not parameters** (Du et al., NeurIPS 2024, arXiv:2403.15796) and **measure per-skill "quanta"** learned in frequency order (Michaud et al., NeurIPS 2023, arXiv:2303.13506). Gives A3 a principled x-axis (capability vs trunk loss) and a natural grain (per-pathway quanta) that ties to A2's per-programme slots.
- **Mechanistic progress measures** track a "sudden" capability as smooth internal circuit formation (Nanda et al., ICLR 2023, arXiv:2301.05217); **grokking** warns biology may generalize only late in training, so early-stopping on C-index could stop before biology "groks" (Power et al., arXiv:2201.02177).

### 2. Reading latent knowledge (ELK) is an open problem with mandatory sanity checks
- **Unsupervised latent-truth probes exist** — Contrast-Consistent Search reads yes/no beliefs from activations, robust to prompts that make the model lie (Burns et al., ICLR 2023, arXiv:2212.03827); truth is often **linearly** represented and causally steerable via difference-in-means probes (Marks & Tegmark, COLM 2024, arXiv:2310.06824); supervised probes on hidden state beat the model's own output probability (Azaria & Mitchell, EMNLP-F 2023, arXiv:2304.13734).
- **BUT unsupervised probes find the most *prominent* feature, not knowledge** — CCS-style methods provably latch onto arbitrary salient/distractor features that also satisfy consistency (Farquhar et al., DeepMind 2023, arXiv:2312.10029). **Implication:** for a tumor trunk, a "latent-biology" probe could be reading a batch/site direction. Any elicitation head must pass Farquhar-style distractor/confound controls.
- **ELK is genuinely unsolved** (Christiano, Cotra, Xu, ARC 2021): a model's overt output may reflect a "human simulator" rather than its direct knowledge. **Implication (direct hazard):** MORPHEUS's closed-RAG hypothesis card may report the *whitelist / plausible narrative*, not the trunk's actual latent biology. Elicitation must target internal state, not the generated card.
- **Models are calibrated about what they know** — P(True)/P(IK) self-evaluation improves with scale (Kadavath et al., Anthropic 2022, arXiv:2207.05221). Feeds A1 abstention and means A3 eval should report *calibration*, not just accuracy.

### 3. Probing must be disciplined or it proves nothing
- **Control tasks + selectivity** — a high-accuracy probe means nothing unless it *fails* on a random-label control (Hewitt & Liang, EMNLP 2019, arXiv:1909.03368). Every "the trunk encodes pathway P" claim must beat a random-programme control.
- **MDL / information-theoretic probing** replaces fragile accuracy with a probe-capacity-robust measure (Voita & Titov, EMNLP 2020, arXiv:2003.12298; Pimentel et al., ACL 2020, arXiv:2004.03061) — resistant to both the metric mirage and over-powered probes. Cleanly separates "biology is present" (MI/identifiability, A2) from "biology is easily promptable" (ease of extraction, A1).
- **Amnesic probing** — encoded ≠ causally used; erase a property (INLP) and measure behavior change (Elazar et al., TACL 2021, arXiv:2006.00995). This bridges A3↔A5: "erase pathway P from the latent — does the tumor-state prediction change?" is far stronger than a correlational probe.
- **Adversarial elicitation validity.** Password-locked models (Greenblatt et al., NeurIPS 2024, arXiv:2405.19550) and sandbagging (van der Weij et al., 2024, arXiv:2406.07358) show absence-on-one-probe ≠ absence: distinguish "the trunk doesn't know biology" from "our prompt failed to elicit it." Motivates multi-format, adversarial elicitation and a locked-capability control.
- **The LAMA lineage** (Petroni et al., EMNLP 2019, arXiv:1909.01066) established cloze knowledge-probing but is notoriously *prompt-sensitive* — the exact fragility MORPHEUS's A1 task auto-detection claims to fix; cite as the baseline to beat.

### 4. The biology-specific nulls that any A3 claim must clear
- **Text priors already encode much "biology."** GenePT — GPT-3.5 text-embeddings of gene descriptions rival/beat Geneformer & scGPT with no omics pretraining (Chen & Zou, Nat. Biomed. Eng. 2024). **This is the flagship A3 adversary:** MORPHEUS must prove its multimodal state adds knowledge *not present in text priors* — a decisive experiment, not an assertion.
- **Zero-shot single-cell FMs often fail to beat PCA** on clustering/integration (Kedzierska et al., bioRxiv 2023.10.16.561085); logistic regression ties FMs on annotation (Boiarsky et al., bioRxiv 2023); deep perturbation predictors don't beat a linear mean-shift (Ahlmann-Eltze et al., bioRxiv 2024). The field *lacks* a credible emergent-biology metric and current FMs fail naive ones — this is A3's opening *and* its bar.
- **Emergence-from-scale is expected**, so A3 novelty must isolate emergence attributable to the *identified/multimodal design*, not scale (C2S-Scale, Rizvi et al., bioRxiv 2025.04.14.648850).
- **The elicitation-evaluation methodology exists only for clinical *text*** — Med-PaLM's multi-axis rubric (factuality/harm/bias) measures *elicited* knowledge, not downstream accuracy (Singhal et al., Nature 2023). Porting a multi-axis elicitation eval to *emergent mechanistic/pathway* knowledge is genuinely unoccupied.

### 5. Grounding is also a confound defense — but the "unified space" is fractured
- **Language bottleneck de-confounds.** Routing patches through an intermediate textual description before embedding anchors the vector to biology-language, not pixel-level batch artifacts (GLMP, arXiv:2606.28697) — NL⇄biology grounding used as a batch-robustness mechanism, exactly A3 territory.
- **Site/scanner/batch confounds are pervasive and survive normalization** (Howard et al., Nat. Commun. 2021; de Jong et al., Robustness Index, arXiv:2501.18055; Kömen et al., arXiv:2411.05489). Any "emergent biology" readout must be shown *not* to be the site signature; mandates leave-site-out splits and an RI-style scalar to report.
- **Biomarker co-dependence** means a single-biomarker "prompt" returns a confounded bundle unless slots are disentangled (Dawood et al., Nat. Biomed. Eng. 2026) — the identifiability argument in evaluation form.
- **Grounding quality is measurable, not assertable** — alignment/uniformity on the hypersphere (Wang & Isola, ICML 2020, arXiv:2005.10242) give closed-form NL⇄biology grounding metrics; the **modality gap** (Liang et al., NeurIPS 2022, arXiv:2203.02053) warns the biology-vs-language shared space is geometrically fractured by construction and will silently degrade prompting.

## What this implies for MORPHEUS design (build-on vs new-design)

**Build-on (prior art — cite as mechanism, do not claim):**
- The *readout* machinery: CCS / difference-in-means linear probes, SAPLMA supervised probes, RepE read/steer vectors — reading latent knowledge from activations is prior art. MORPHEUS claims the *biological, multimodal, per-pathway* instantiation, not "reading activations."
- The *probing discipline*: selectivity/control tasks, MDL, amnesic INLP — adopt wholesale as the harness backbone.
- The *elicitation-eval frame*: Med-PaLM's multi-axis rubric — port it from clinical text to mechanistic biology.
- The *nulls*: GenePT text-prior baseline, Kedzierska PCA/HVG baseline, linear mean-shift — pre-register these as the bar.

**New-design (the defensible A3 whitespace):**
The field has **no confound-aware, validity-certified emergent-biological-knowledge benchmark for a multimodal cancer FM** that (a) separates elicited biology from text priors, scale, and site confounds, (b) reports probe-capacity-robust + metric-robust scores, and (c) certifies the probe reads *biology*, not the most-prominent distractor. That benchmark — and the grounding interface it validates — is the axis's core contribution. It is an *evaluation protocol and elicitation head*, not a new trunk; this keeps it orthogonal to the (pre-empted) "promptable multimodal model" and "frozen trunk" claims.

**Hard guardrails to foreground (or a reviewer sinks the claim):** the metric-mirage (report smooth + thresholded, loss-indexed), the Farquhar distractor problem (certify the direction is biology, not site), the modality gap (measure it, don't assume a shared space), and the GenePT null (beat text priors decisively).

## Candidate research directions

### D1. BioELK-Bench: a confound-aware emergent-biology elicitation benchmark with a validity certificate
**Claim.** A curated battery that measures how much *pathway/mechanism* knowledge a frozen multimodal tumor trunk encodes and how readily NL prompting elicits it, scored so the result survives the four killer critiques. Each probe reports: (i) MDL / MI (probe-capacity-robust) *and* thresholded accuracy under both smooth and hard metrics (Voita, Pimentel, Schaeffer); (ii) Hewitt–Liang **selectivity** vs a random-programme control; (iii) a **Farquhar distractor/site sanity check** — the recovered direction must fail to predict submitting-site/scanner (Howard, de Jong RI) while succeeding on biology; (iv) a **decisive delta vs the GenePT text-prior baseline** and the Kedzierska PCA baseline; (v) a **password-locked control** (Greenblatt) to separate "trunk lacks the knowledge" from "prompt failed to elicit it." Emergence is plotted **loss-indexed** (Du), at **per-pathway quantum** grain (Michaud), across multiple elicitation formats (anti-sandbagging).
**Why novel.** Every ingredient is borrowed, but no cited system assembles a metric-robust, confound-certified, text-prior-beating elicitation battery for a *multimodal cancer* FM. Kedzierska/Boiarsky/Ahlmann-Eltze show the field lacks it and that current FMs fail naive versions; Med-PaLM's elicitation rubric covers only clinical text. The *validity certificate* (probe reads biology, not the most-prominent site/distractor feature) is itself an unfilled contribution tying A3 evaluation to A2 identifiability.

### D2. Amnesic-counterfactual causal elicitation: elicited ≠ used, and a validity gate for hypothesis cards
**Claim.** Move A3 from correlational probing to causal elicitation: locate a pathway's latent direction, then **erase it (INLP/amnesic) and measure whether the tumor-state / survival / drug-response prediction causally changes** (Elazar), and dually **steer along it** (RepE, Geometry-of-Truth) to confirm causal sufficiency. Use this as a **validity gate on the closed-RAG hypothesis card**: the card's biological claims are accepted only if they match directions the trunk *causally uses*, directly defusing the ELK "human-simulator" hazard (Christiano) where the card reports the whitelist rather than the trunk's latent state.
**Why novel.** Amnesic probing, ELK, and read/steer vectors are prior art in NLP, and Geneformer does in-silico deletion in gene space — but none provide a *causal-use validity gate on a generated biological explanation* for a multimodal tumor trunk. It converts the closed-RAG card from a fluency risk (fluent-but-wrong, cf. Prot2Text) into a certifiable readout, and operationally links A3 (elicitation) to A5 (intervention) and A2 (identifiability) with a single mechanism.

### D3. The "beyond priors and beyond scale" decisive experiment for emergent multimodal biology
**Claim.** A pre-registered, single experiment that isolates biological knowledge attributable to the *identified multimodal design* rather than to text priors or scale. Three matched arms probed identically: (a) the multimodal identified trunk; (b) a **GenePT/Cell2Sentence text-only prior** with equivalent parameter/loss budget; (c) a **scale-matched non-identified trunk** (loss-indexed per Du, scaling-controlled per C2S-Scale). "Emergent biology" is claimed only for probes where arm (a) exceeds both (b) and (c) by a margin that survives selectivity and site-confound controls, tracked across training to guard against late grokking. Reports the **modality-gap magnitude** (Liang) and **alignment/uniformity** (Wang–Isola) as grounding-quality diagnostics so a positive result cannot be attributed to a collapsed shared space.
**Why novel.** The adjacent literature repeatedly *asserts* emergent biology and is repeatedly falsified by simple baselines; no cited work runs the controlled, loss-indexed, text-prior-vs-scale-vs-design ablation that would make a positive claim defensible. It directly answers the reviewer's inevitable "isn't this just scale/priors?" and turns the field's strongest steelman-the-null critiques (GenePT, Kedzierska, Ahlmann-Eltze) from a vulnerability into MORPHEUS's differentiator.
