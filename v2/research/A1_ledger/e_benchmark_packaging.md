## Sources
- https://mlcommons.org/2024/03/croissant_metadata_announce/ — Croissant 1.0 (Mar 2024): schema.org/JSON-LD dataset card standard adopted by HF, Kaggle, OpenML, TFDS; NeurIPS D&B recommended artifact. Defines "dataset card" layer of packaging.
- https://huggingface.co/docs/dataset-viewer/en/croissant — HF auto-generates Croissant JSON-LD for every hosted dataset; shows the discoverability/portability payoff of HF hosting.
- https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.09548 (Therapeutics Data Commons, NeurIPS 2021) — canonical adopted omics/therapeutics benchmark: 29 "benchmark groups," programmatic Python `get_split(method='scaffold', seed, frac)`, distribution-shift splits, public leaderboards.
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.00687 / https://ogb.stanford.edu (Open Graph Benchmark) — automated data loaders + standardized evaluators + leaderboards; requires reporting validation performance + tuned hyperparameters to prevent test-set over-tuning. Model for eval-harness rigor.
- https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10546459/ + https://miccai.org/sig/sig-challenges/ — med-imaging challenges shifted from releasing test sets to containerized (Docker) submissions run centrally on withheld cases; MICCAI SIG-Challenges pushes reproducibility/transparency. - https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.15383 (MorphoHELM) — confound-aware benchmark with hierarchical batch-effect stringency tiers (Not Same Batch / Not Same Source / Not Same Layout); leaderboard reports per-tier so batch-artifact reliance is exposed. Closest analogue to our confound-aware metric design.
- https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-021-24698-1 (Howard et al. 2021, Nat Commun) — site-specific digital histology signatures in TCGA bias DL prediction of survival, mutations, gene-expression; site detectable despite color norm. ## Findings
- **The adopted-benchmark stack is a small, repeatable set of layers.** Across TDC, OGB, and HF, the recurring components are: (1) programmatic data loader with FIXED, named splits; (2) a standardized evaluator that computes the metric identically for everyone; (3) a public leaderboard; (4) a machine-readable dataset card. TDC exposes this as `get_split(method='scaffold', seed=1, frac=[.7,.1,.2])`; OGB ships loaders+evaluators+leaderboards as one package. [TDC; OGB]
- **"Splits that mimic real-world distribution shift" is a first-class adoption feature, not an afterthought.** TDC deliberately uses scaffold splits to enforce structural novelty in the test set; OGB uses domain/time splits. The split IS the scientific claim of the benchmark. [TDC; OGB]
- **Leaderboard rigor prevents test-set overfitting by protocol, not trust.** OGB mandates reporting validation performance + tuned hyperparameters in every submission to discourage models over-tuned to the public test set. This is a cheap, code-free confound guard. [OGB]
- **Held-out test servers are now standard in med-imaging and increasingly enforced via containers.** Grand Challenge / MICCAI moved from handing participants the test set to requiring Docker submissions run centrally on withheld cases — cited as the single biggest lever on challenge quality and reproducibility. [PMC10546459; MICCAI SIG]
- **The HF private-test-set pattern is the minimum viable held-out server.** Four objects: public leaderboard Space (submit), private evaluator Space (holds gold labels, runs metric), private submissions dataset (predictions only, never labels), public results dataset (scores only). Leakage prevention is by access control + unidirectional data flow, not cryptography. This is buildable by one lab without a competition platform. [HF hugging-science]
- **Croissant (MLCommons, Mar 2024) is the emerging standard "dataset card" and is now a NeurIPS D&B recommended artifact.** JSON-LD on schema.org, auto-generated by HF for every dataset, loadable by TFDS/PyTorch/JAX, and indexed by Google Dataset Search — this is the discoverability layer that turns a hosted dataset into an adoptable one. [MLCommons Croissant; HF Croissant docs]
- **Confound-aware evaluation already has a concrete, adopted template: hierarchical stringency tiers with per-tier leaderboard reporting.** MorphoHELM evaluates biological retrieval at Not-Same-Batch / Not-Same-Source / Not-Same-Layout stringency; the falsifiable design claim is that a model strong only at the loosest tier is riding batch artifacts, not biology. This is structurally identical to our global vs within-cancer vs random-control decomposition. [MorphoHELM]
- **The specific confound our benchmark quantifies (cross-cancer / tissue-of-origin structure inflating WSI->molecular scores) is independently established as a real bias.** Howard 2021 shows TCGA tissue-submitting-site signatures survive color normalization and bias DL prediction of gene expression, mutations, and survival; separate work shows between-tissue differences make "substantial contributions" to pan-cancer prediction and that excluding them drops performance. So the direction and rough magnitude of our confound is corroborated. [Howard 2021 Nat Commun; pan-cancer between-tissue work]
- **Current WSI->gene-expression benchmarks report raw per-gene correlation and do NOT package the confound controls.** The 2026 foundation-model gene-expression benchmark and the 2025 cross-modal histology<->expression benchmark report per-gene Spearman/attention-MIL results but neither structures its headline metric around within-cancer stratification NOR reports a random-gene-set negative control. This is the packaging gap our lane fills. [biorxiv 2026 709012; arXiv 2508.01490]
- **Repository-level adoption requirements (FAIR, versioned dataset cards, license, maintenance plan) are documented but orthogonal to the scientific novelty** — necessary hygiene, not differentiators. [Benchmark Data Repositories 2410.24100]

## Novelty verdict
Our finding is **partially known on the confound existence, but appears novel on the packaged, quantified decomposition + control adjustment as a benchmark metric.**

Reasoning:
- The *existence* of the confound is prior art. Howard 2021 and the pan-cancer between-tissue literature already establish that tissue-of-origin / site structure inflates histology->molecular prediction and survives normalization. So "WSI->molecular prompting is confounded by cross-cancer cohort structure" is NOT novel as a qualitative claim.
- The *evaluation template* is prior art in an adjacent domain. MorphoHELM already operationalizes confound-aware evaluation via hierarchical stringency tiers with per-tier leaderboard reporting — the same idea as our global -> within-cancer -> random-control ladder. So the meta-method is not novel in the abstract.
- What appears **novel** is the specific, falsifiable, packaged quantification for WSI->molecular-programme (Hallmark) prompting: (a) that ~46-49% of global Pearson is cross-cancer cohort structure (0.348 -> 0.188 within-cancer); (b) that a random-gene-set control already scores ~0.30-0.32 globally; and critically (c) that the genuine within-cancer, random-control-ADJUSTED specificity collapses to ~+0.07 Pearson for EVERY method including the baseline — i.e., the confound-adjusted signal is near-floor and method-invariant. No current WSI->molecular benchmark (the 2026 foundation-model and 2025 cross-modal benchmarks) reports either the within-cancer stratification or the random-gene negative control, let alone the combined adjustment. The claim that method rankings essentially vanish under joint confound+control adjustment (with SigLIP's +0.005 within-cancer edge being the only residual) is the novel, publishable core and is exactly the kind of result the confound-aware packaging is designed to surface.

Net: the confound is known; the confound-aware benchmarking pattern exists elsewhere; but the quantified, control-adjusted, method-invariance result for WSI->Hallmark prompting — and its packaging as the benchmark's headline metric — is not present in the sources found and appears novel. Recommended MVP packaging to make it adoptable: TDC/OGB-style loader with a fixed within-cancer split + a mandatory random-gene-set control run + a Croissant dataset card + an HF private-evaluator held-out server, with the leaderboard reporting the control-adjusted within-cancer delta (not raw global Pearson) as the primary number.
