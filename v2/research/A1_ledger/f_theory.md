## Sources
- https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2203.07004 — Wang et al., "Rethinking Minimal Sufficient Representation in Contrastive Learning" (CVPR 2022). The load-bearing formal result: Theorem 1 shows the alignment objective drives representations toward the *minimal sufficient* representation, which provably discards non-shared task-relevant information I(v1,T|v2). This is the direct information-theoretic statement behind our biology-head collapse.
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.05268 — Liang et al., "Factorized Contrastive Learning: Going Beyond Multi-view Redundancy" (NeurIPS 2023). Standard cross-modal contrastive learning captures ONLY shared (redundant) information; task-relevant info in modality-unique regions is lost unless representation is explicitly factorized into shared/unique blocks.
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.10242 — Wang & Isola, "Alignment and Uniformity on the Hypersphere" (ICML 2020). Formalizes contrastive geometry as two competing terms (alignment vs uniformity) and shows they cannot both be perfectly satisfied — the base geometric tradeoff that a retrieval-tuned latent optimizes.
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.09348 — Jing et al., "Understanding Dimensional Collapse in Contrastive Self-supervised Learning" (ICLR 2022). Contrastive embeddings collapse to a low-rank subspace (few dominant singular values); mechanistic account of the effective-rank collapse we observe.
- https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.04906 — Bardes et al., VICReg. Variance term is PER-DIMENSION (hinge on std of each coordinate); it does NOT prevent collapse on its own — the off-diagonal covariance term is what actually enforces rank. Directly explains why our per-dim variance floor (0.01) failed to stop rank collapse.
- https://arxiv.org/pdf/2401.10474 — Huang et al., "LDReg: Local Dimensionality Regularized SSL" (ICLR 2024). Effective/local dimensionality of the representation governs downstream capacity; higher effective dim → better downstream. Ties rank directly to prediction capacity.
- https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1012952 — "Geometry of efficient codes: rate-distortion trade-offs distort latent representations" (PLOS Comp Biol 2025). At low capacity, rate-distortion-optimal codes orthogonalize and collapse onto task-relevant axes only — a rate-distortion framing of head collapse.
- https://arxiv.org/html/2411.01144 and https://medium.com/@eezarbali/how-contrastive-learning-works-in-regression-tasks-99b9df3593fa — contrastive positive/negative structure is ill-defined for continuous/regression targets (no hard class boundary), causing over-dispersion / loss of local smoothness needed for regression.
- https://arxiv.org/pdf/2304.09355 — Shwartz-Ziv & LeCun, "To Compress or Not to Compress: SSL and Information Theory" (review). Frames SSL as a compression-vs-sufficiency problem; over-compression to shared info hurts tasks needing the discarded bits.

## Findings
- **Theorem 1 (Wang et al. CVPR 2022) is the clean proposition we want.** For two views v1,v2 and downstream task T:
  I(v1,T) = I(z1^min,T) + I(v1,T|v2)  ≥  I(z1^suf,T)  ≥  I(z1^min,T).
  The alignment/InfoNCE objective (maximize I(z1,z2)) drives the encoder toward the *minimal sufficient* representation z1^min, which by construction retains only cross-view-shared task information and provably discards the non-shared term I(v1,T|v2). Theorem 2 shows this raises the Bayes-error bound whenever non-shared task-relevant info exists. Mapping to us: WSI = v1, Hallmark-programme text = v2. The biology head aligned to the shared WSI-Hallmark manifold and discarded exactly the WSI-resident molecular-regression variance that is NOT predictable from the coarse Hallmark prompt — the +0.07 residual specificity ceiling.
- **FactorCL (NeurIPS 2023): a single contrastive latent captures only multi-view *redundancy*.** The assumption "shared info is necessary and sufficient for the task" is generally false; task info also lives in modality-unique regions. Optimality for a broad task requires factorizing into shared + unique blocks — i.e., you cannot serve both retrieval (which lives in the shared block) and full regression (which needs unique blocks) from ONE aligned latent. This is the multi-view-redundancy statement of our result.
- **Alignment-uniformity is an intrinsic geometric tradeoff (Wang & Isola 2020).** Retrieval quality is driven by uniformity (spread on the hypersphere); perfect alignment and perfect uniformity are not simultaneously achievable. A latent tuned for retrieval geometry spends capacity on angular uniformity, not on preserving the metric structure a regressor needs. This is the "retrieval geometry vs regression capacity" tension stated abstractly.
- **Dimensional collapse (Jing et al. ICLR 2022) is the mechanism of our effective-rank ~5-6.** Contrastive alignment along augmentation/target directions produces low-rank solutions where a few singular values dominate. LDReg (ICLR 2024) shows effective dimensionality bounds downstream capacity — so a collapsed head (rank 5-6 of 256) is capacity-starved for 50-D Hallmark regression by construction, while the healthy identity head (rank ~84) is not.
- **Our VICReg-floor failure is textbook, not a bug.** VICReg's variance term is per-coordinate; the original paper is explicit that variance alone does not prevent collapse — the OFF-DIAGONAL covariance term is what forces the dimensions to be decorrelated and thus full-rank. Our floor was per-dimension (weight 0.01) with no covariance/decorrelation term, so the head was free to collapse to a rank-5-6 subspace while each retained coordinate still cleared its per-dim variance hinge. This is a precise, independently-predicted explanation of the mechanism, not merely a post-hoc analogy.
- **Contrastive/supcon structure is ill-posed for continuous regression targets.** With continuous Hallmark scores there is no clean positive/negative boundary; supcon-style objectives over-disperse and destroy the local metric smoothness a regressor exploits. This corroborates that the SAME objective that builds good retrieval neighborhoods is actively counterproductive for the regression head.
- **Rate-distortion framing (PLOS 2025 + Shwartz-Ziv/LeCun review): collapse is the optimal code under a capacity/rate constraint.** When capacity is constrained, the rate-distortion-optimal representation orthogonalizes and collapses onto task-relevant axes, discarding everything else. A shared latent under a retrieval distortion measure is thus *expected* to shed the molecular-regression subspace — collapse is optimal for the wrong distortion.
- **Multi-task/gradient-conflict evidence supports separate heads.** Shared trunks under conflicting objectives settle into a compromise suboptimal for each task; head weights become near-orthogonal so tasks occupy non-overlapping subspaces. Supports the architectural conclusion that retrieval and regression should not share one latent.

## Novelty verdict
Our *general* claim — "a single shared contrastive latent trades off retrieval geometry against downstream regression capacity" — is **already reported** at the theory level, and strongly so: Wang et al. (CVPR 2022) Theorem 1 gives the exact information-theoretic inequality (alignment → minimal sufficient → discards I(v1,T|v2)), FactorCL (NeurIPS 2023) gives the shared-vs-unique factorization argument, Wang & Isola give the alignment-uniformity geometric tradeoff, and dimensional-collapse + LDReg tie effective rank to downstream capacity. We should CITE these rather than claim the proposition as novel; a clean stated proposition for the paper can be assembled directly from Theorem 1 + FactorCL.

What is **partially known / appears novel** is the *specific empirical instantiation and quantification* in the WSI→molecular-programme setting:
1. A concrete measurement of the discarded term: effective rank collapse to ~5-6 of 256 on the biology head while a parallel identity head stays healthy at ~84 — an unusually clean, matched-pair demonstration of the theorem inside one model. Not, to our search, reported for pathology/molecular WSI heads.
2. The confound decomposition (global 0.348 → within-cancer 0.188; random-gene-set control ~0.30-0.32; genuine within-cancer, control-adjusted specificity only ~+0.07 for EVERY method including baseline) is an empirical contribution the theory literature does not supply — it quantifies exactly how little non-shared task signal the shared latent could ever have captured, and shows the ceiling binds all methods.
3. The precise VICReg-mechanism diagnosis — that a *per-dimension* variance floor (0.01) without a covariance/decorrelation term is insufficient to prevent rank collapse of an alignment-regressed head — is a correct application of known VICReg theory to explain a specific failure, and is a useful, publishable mechanistic finding even though the underlying VICReg property is established.

Bottom line: the theory is prior art (cite Wang-CVPR22, FactorCL, Wang-Isola, Jing-ICLR22, VICReg); our contribution is the sharp, matched-head empirical confirmation plus the confound-adjusted specificity ceiling and the concrete variance-floor-vs-covariance diagnosis. Frame the paper's proposition as a corollary of Theorem 1, not as a new theorem.
