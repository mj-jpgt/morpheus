## Findings
- **The confound is already a named, published concern (strong hit).** Buyer Beware (Nat BME 2026) argues broadly that molecular-biomarker-from-histology accuracy is inflated by co-dependent clinicopathological structure and that the true marginal signal over trivially-inferable features is modest. Our claim "46-49% of Pearson is cross-cancer cohort structure; genuine within-cancer specificity ~+0.07" is a quantitative instance of a thesis this paper already asserts qualitatively.
- **A CLIP-specific, TCGA-specific version already exists (strongest hit).** DECAT (arXiv 2026) shows CLIP-style entangled embeddings falsely claim shared biology in 92% of confounded TCGA cases and that within-cancer stratification collapses AUROC (e.g. -0.24 TMB). This mirrors both halves of our finding: (i) a CLIP/contrastive representation manufacturing apparent signal, and (ii) within-cancer stratification revealing it. An adversary can argue our result is a re-derivation of DECAT's on a different label set (Hallmark programmes).
- **Stratified / site-preserved splits are established practice, positioned as the fix.** Howard 2021 quantifies the confound (mean -0.069 AUROC, 36% of features vanish) and gives preserved-site CV as the remedy. Reviewer line: "the correct evaluation is already known; you rediscovered why you must stratify, and stratification already solves it."
- **Within-cancer evaluation is not novel methodology.** SEQUOIA (2024) already builds per-cancer models and uses permutation-based significance; random/permuted controls and per-cohort splits are routine. Our random-gene-set control (~0.30-0.32 global) is a variant of controls the field already runs.
- **Rank collapse is a textbook, fixable bug.** Dimensional collapse under contrastive/alignment losses is a named phenomenon (Jing 2022) with standard fixes (VICReg variance floor, covariance/orthogonality reg). An adversary: "effective rank 5-6 of 256 is a mis-tuned regularizer (per-dim 0.01 variance floor was too weak / mis-scaled), i.e. a hyperparameter bug, not a scientific result." The finding that a per-dimension variance floor fails to prevent collapse is even anticipated by VICReg-critique work noting full rank is 'difficult in practice' and that per-dim variance is insufficient without the covariance term.
- **The ~0.07 specific-signal number can be spun to sink the task itself.** Benchmarking (2025) shows methods routinely sit at low PCC; combined with Buyer Beware's "modest gain over grade," an adversary argues WSI->Hallmark-programme prompting has so little genuine within-cancer signal (~+0.07 for every method incl. baseline) that the negative result is unsurprising and unpublishable — "task is known to be near-uninteresting, you confirmed it."
- **SigLIP edge is negligible.** +0.005 within-cancer / 62% target win-rate is well inside the noise band the benchmarking literature reports (~0.2 variability), so an adversary discounts the one positive comparative result as not robust.

## Novelty verdict
**PARTIALLY KNOWN, with two components at real risk of "already reported."**

Against the steelman:
- The *general confound* (cross-cancer / tissue-of-origin / cohort structure inflating histology->molecular performance) is unambiguously prior art — Buyer Beware and Howard establish it, including the direction and the recommended fix (stratified / site-preserved splits). Our contribution here is quantitative refinement, not conceptual discovery.
- The *CLIP-manufactures-apparent-shared-biology + within-cancer-collapse* pattern is closely anticipated by DECAT (92% false shared-biology on TCGA CLIP; within-cancer AUROC collapse). This is the most dangerous overlap: an adversary can credibly claim our mechanism is a re-instantiation of DECAT on Hallmark targets.
- *Rank collapse as a phenomenon* is fully prior art; if our headline is "the biology head collapsed," that alone is a known, fixable bug.

Where the steelman does NOT fully sink us (residual novelty the null case cannot erase):
1. **Specific, decomposed magnitude on a Hallmark-programme prompting benchmark.** No cited source reports the exact decomposition: global 0.348 -> within-cancer 0.188, random-gene control 0.30-0.32, net specificity ~+0.07 identical across *every* method including baseline, on WSI->Hallmark prompting. DECAT works on classification AUROC over TMB/Age/TP53; Buyer Beware on discrete biomarkers; none report a random-gene-set-adjusted within-cancer Pearson floor for molecular-programme prompting.
2. **The mechanistic coupling is not in the prior art.** Prior work documents the confound OR documents dimensional collapse, but no cited source ties them together: that aligning a 256-D head to a ~50-D Hallmark manifold via neighbour-KL + supcon *causes* rank collapse to ~5-6, that identity stays healthy (~84) with anchor residual ~0, and that a per-dimension VICReg variance floor (0.01) demonstrably fails to prevent it in this setting. That is a specific negative mechanistic result, not merely "collapse happens."

Net: the *framing* "we discovered the task is confounded" is NOT novel and the steelman defeats it. The defensible contribution is narrower and must be pitched as: (i) a precise, control-adjusted quantification of residual within-cancer signal (~+0.07) on the Hallmark-prompting benchmark specifically, and (ii) the mechanistic demonstration that the alignment objective + weak per-dim variance floor drives biology-head rank collapse while identity is preserved. If the paper leads with the confound-as-discovery, reviewers armed with Buyer Beware / DECAT / Howard will sink it; if it leads with the coupled mechanism + benchmark-specific decomposition, the steelman leaves a real but modest surviving contribution.
