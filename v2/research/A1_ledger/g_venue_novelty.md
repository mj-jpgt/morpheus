## Sources
- https://www.nature.com/articles/s41551-026-01616-8 — "Confounding factors and biases abound when predicting molecular biomarkers from histological images" (Nat. Biomed. Eng., published version of "Buyer Beware"). THE closest confound paper: shows biomarker-biomarker and clinicopathological dependencies confound WSI->omics prediction; accuracy varies with codependent biomarker status; gains over what a pathologist infers from grade are modest.
- https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2024.06.23.600257v1 — preprint of the above ("Buyer Beware"). Method: conditional-independence principle + permutation testing + biomarker-stratified subgroup evaluation. Recommends codependency-aware target definition and stringent stratification testing.
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.01490 — HESCAPE: large-scale benchmark of cross-modal contrastive pretraining for histology<->gene expression (spatial transcriptomics, pan-organ, 6 gene panels, 54 donors). Finds contrastive pretraining IMPROVES mutation classification but DEGRADES direct expression prediction vs baseline encoders; gene-expression encoder drives alignment; batch effects interfere with cross-modal alignment.
- https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.08203 — "A Theoretical Framework for Preventing Class Collapse in Supervised Contrastive Learning": SupCon can drive within-class embeddings to extremely low effective rank while retaining classification accuracy; explicit variance/decorrelation (orthogonality) regularization maintaining within-class effective rank prevents it. Directly names the effective-rank collapse phenomenon.
- https://openreview.net/pdf?id=YevsQ05DEN7 — "Understanding Dimensional Collapse in Contrastive Learning": embeddings collapse to low-dim subspace via strong augmentation + implicit low-rank weight bias, even with negatives.
- https://arxiv.org/pdf/2403.18699 — VICReg-family analysis: variance floor + covariance decorrelation needed to prevent collapse; full-rank hard to achieve in practice with variance term alone.
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.15343 — SigLIP (sigmoid loss): pairwise sigmoid beats softmax CLIP at moderate batch sizes; per-pair scoring encourages finer-grained discrimination. Explains a plausible mechanism for SigLIP's small win in our data.
- https://www.cell.com/fulltext/S2405-4712(15)00218-5 — MSigDB Hallmark gene set collection (defines the ~50 Hallmark programmes we prompt against); random-signature/label permutation is the established significance control in the gene-set-enrichment world but is applied to enrichment scores, not to WSI-prompting specificity.

## Findings
- [Nat. Biomed. Eng. / Buyer Beware] Confounding in WSI->molecular biomarker prediction is an established, publishable concern in a top venue: predictive quality is inflated by biomarker interdependencies and clinicopathological structure, and genuine isolated-biomarker signal is much smaller than headline numbers. BUT their confounder axis is biomarker co-occurrence/clinicopath variables, controlled via conditional-independence stratification + permutation. They do NOT do a cancer-type variance decomposition of Pearson (global vs within-cancer) nor a random-gene-set specificity floor for molecular-programme prompting. [nature.com/articles/s41551-026-01616-8; biorxiv 2024.06.23.600257v1]
- [HESCAPE] The "contrastive/alignment objective degrades direct expression prediction" paradox is already reported, and batch effect is already named as the confounder interfering with alignment. This partially anticipates our "alignment head is worse / confounded" story, but HESCAPE is spatial-transcriptomics contrastive pretraining, does not decompose Pearson into cross-cancer vs within-cancer, uses no random-gene-set control, and does not analyze effective-rank collapse of a biology head. [arxiv.org/abs/2508.01490]
- [Class-collapse theory] Our mechanism — a 256-D head driven by supcon + neighbour-KL toward a ~50-D target manifold collapses to effective rank ~5-6, and a per-dimension variance floor (VICReg-style, 0.01) fails to prevent it — is exactly the failure mode the SupCon class-collapse and dimensional-collapse literature predicts. It is theoretically known that (a) SupCon collapses within-class rank, (b) a naive per-dimension variance floor is insufficient (covariance/decorrelation needed). So the mechanism is NOT novel as phenomenology; the novelty is empirical instantiation + the specific diagnosis in a multi-head WSI-molecular model. [arxiv 2503.08203; openreview YevsQ05DEN7; arxiv 2403.18699]
- [Effective-rank contrast] The clean finding that in the SAME model the identity head stays healthy (rank ~84, anchor residual ~0 to frozen MLP-CLIP teacher) while the biology head collapses (rank ~5-6) is a per-head, per-objective collapse contrast that I found NO direct prior report of. The literature studies single-objective collapse; a within-model "one head collapses, its sibling doesn't, attributable to the auxiliary objective family" comparison appears novel.
- [SigLIP] SigLIP beating CLIP-softmax at moderate batch is expected from the sigmoid-loss paper; our +0.005 within-cancer / 62%-of-targets win is consistent with, not contradicted by, prior art. Low novelty on its own.
- [Random-gene-set control] Random-signature permutation is standard in GSEA/gene-signature significance, but I found no WSI->molecular-programme PROMPTING benchmark that uses a random-gene-set score as a specificity floor and reports that every method (incl. baseline) clears it by only ~+0.07 within-cancer Pearson. That specific control+result framing appears novel in the pathology-foundation-model benchmarking venue.

## Novelty verdict
Overall: APPEARS NOVEL as a package, with two components partially anticipated.

- Confound-aware WSI->molecular benchmark (contribution i): PARTIALLY KNOWN in spirit. "Buyer Beware"/Nat. Biomed. Eng. already legitimizes and publishes the confounding critique, and HESCAPE already shows contrastive alignment degrades expression prediction and names batch as confounder. A reviewer WILL cite these and expect them discussed. HOWEVER our specific contributions are not in prior art: (1) decomposing Pearson into cross-cancer cohort structure (~46-49% of signal; global 0.348 -> within-cancer 0.188) as the dominant confounder for HALLMARK-PROGRAMME prompting specifically; (2) a random-gene-set specificity floor (~0.30-0.32 global) yielding a genuine within-cancer, control-adjusted specificity of only ~+0.07 Pearson for EVERY method including the baseline. That "the residual real signal is tiny and method-invariant" result is a sharper, more falsifiable claim than the existing qualitative critiques. Reviewer verdict: novel enough IF positioned as a quantitative, control-adjusted refinement of Buyer Beware/HESCAPE rather than the first to notice confounding.

- Multi-objective / rank-collapse analysis (contribution ii): mechanism is KNOWN (SupCon class collapse; per-dimension variance floor insufficient without decorrelation), but the empirical, within-model per-head collapse contrast (biology rank ~5-6 vs identity ~84 in one network, tied to neighbour-KL+supcon aligning to a ~50-D Hallmark manifold, with a real anchor-residual~0 sanity check) APPEARS NOVEL as a concrete diagnostic. Reviewer verdict: the diagnosis is novel; frame it as "we show the textbook collapse failure actually fires in a deployed WSI-molecular alignment head and explains the benchmark's method-invariance," and cite 2503.08203 / YevsQ05DEN7 / VICReg to preempt "this is known."

Strongest defensible novelty claim: a random-gene-set-adjusted, within-cancer decomposition showing the genuine WSI->Hallmark specificity is only ~+0.07 Pearson and is method-invariant, MECHANISTICALLY explained by objective-induced rank collapse of the biology head (~5-6 of 256) that a per-dimension VICReg floor failed to prevent — a benchmark+mechanism pairing not present in Buyer Beware, HESCAPE, or the collapse-theory papers.

Best-fit venues: NeurIPS Datasets & Benchmarks or MICCAI (benchmark + negative-control framing); Nature Communications / Nature Biomedical Engineering if paired with the mechanism (Buyer Beware precedent shows NBME appetite for this critique). ICLR viable if the rank-collapse mechanism is made the headline. Main reviewer risk: "Buyer Beware and HESCAPE already said confounding/alignment-degradation exists" — mitigated by leading with the quantitative decomposition + random-gene-set floor + per-head collapse contrast, none of which those papers report.
