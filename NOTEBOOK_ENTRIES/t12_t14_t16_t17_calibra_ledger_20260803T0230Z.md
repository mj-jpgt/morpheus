**T1.7(c) — synthetic spike recovery. PASSES.** `transmission_floor = 0.01` (the finest level on the

consistent with adjustment removing variance that was diluting the channel.

### In plain terms

Everything we can check about whether the pipeline is honest, checks out. If you pair one patient's
slide with another patient's RNA, the signal collapses — and out of two thousand such scramblings, not
one came near the real number. If you plant a fake signal of known strength, the pipeline delivers it
back at essentially full strength. Randomly chosen gene sets do not clear the detection threshold.

But there is a large, uncomfortable number in here. When we ask the image to predict one gene set at a
time using a fitted direction, a *deliberately random* gene set — one matched only on how variable its
genes are and how they load on the biggest axis of expression — scores about 77% of what a real
curated pathway scores. So the channel is real (it beats scrambling, decisively) but it is only about a
quarter specific to the biology we name. Most of what the images read is generic expression structure
that many different gene sets share.

### Meaning for the claim

* **P1:** the battery's must-FAIL and must-PASS controls all behave. T1.6 is now at 2,000 permutations,
  which is what a paper number needs; the 1/2001 resolution is quoted with every p.
* **The 0.147 null median is a required companion to every channel number on this project.** A
  16-component top-CCA on 2,766 patients cannot go below it. Quoting 0.42 without it overstates the
  effect by a factor a reviewer will find immediately.
* **P3 — second independent blow.** Random covariate-matched gene sets at 76–82% of real gene sets is
  the T1.5 result again from a different direction. Any per-gene-set legibility claim must now be made
  as a *difference* against the matched random control, with a CI, not as an absolute correlation.
* **P2:** the chance level for the rank-vs-channel comparison is now measured: null median 0.147, null
  p95 0.164–0.169.
* **Not done in this run:** the text-prior (GenePT-style) and capacity-matched cell-composition target
  blocks of T1.1 need external resources (a gene text-embedding table; a deconvolution signature
  matrix) that are on neither machine. They are the two the plan flagged as most likely to slip, and
  they slipped. The zero-parameter cancer-type-mean baseline is degenerate on this split by
  construction — the maximal split holds out whole cancers, so every test patient receives the global
  training mean and the state is constant on the evaluated partition. That is a property of the split,
  not a bug, and it means the zero-parameter comparison is only meaningful on `--partition all`.
