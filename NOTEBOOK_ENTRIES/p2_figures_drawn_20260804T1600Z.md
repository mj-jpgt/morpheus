### The discipline that made it worth doing

**No script carries a figure value as a literal.** Every number is read at draw time from a vendored
box artifact under `figures/data/` or from a markdown source in this repository. `data/` is written
only by `extract_from_box.py`, which pulls in one `tar` stream — never per-file `scp` — and records
every file in `data/MANIFEST.json` with its box path, size and SHA-256; a test re-hashes the tree
against that manifest.

Each script then cross-checks its sources against **one another** and stops rather than plotting
either when they disagree. F2 recomputes the variance decomposition from the twelve per-artifact
metrics and asserts it equals the printed decomposition in `~/ws_p2/out/p2_run.log` — it reproduces
34.5 / 29.1 / 98.0 and F(3,8) = 1.41 / 1.09 / 128.20 to the digit. T1 recomputes every selection mark
the metrics files can support, and recomputes the exact two-sided binomial *p* from `math.comb`
rather than copying 0.219 / 0.688. F6 reads its pre-declared thresholds from the script that declares
them (`RANK_FOLD = 2.0`, `CCA_DELTA = 0.0705`) and cross-checks them against the notebook entry that
records them as fixed before the pair list was inspected.

Two parser bugs were caught by writing the guards rather than by inspecting the output:

1. **`nums()` had to normalise U+2212 MINUS SIGN.** The markdown sources are typeset with a real
   minus. Without the normalisation, F1(c)'s paired channel differences parse as their **absolute
   values** — the figure would have shown PBS *above* Hallmark on all three seeds, with the CIs
   mirrored. The fix is in `p2fig.nums`, with a test.
2. **A range dash is not a minus.** `DILUTION_LOWER_BOUND.md` writes the permutation null as
   "0.145–0.147"; running that through the same normaliser yields `[0.145, -0.147]`. F7 matches the
   two endpoints positively instead, and asserts both are in (0, 1).

And one source-selection bug: `D2_RESULT.md` carries **three** tables of shape `| seed | ... |`
(unrestricted, stratified, contamination). F1(c) is scoped to the STRATIFIED section by name, and
asserts the direction of the difference it reads, so a source edit that reversed it would fail rather
than flip the figure.

### What could not be drawn honestly

**F1(d), the controlled retraining repeat, is a loud PENDING placeholder.** The five same-seed
repeats under `~/e0_run/d1_envelope/` are running — 16 of 40 epochs logged, 0 of 5 exported. The panel
names the measurement, the path its data will arrive at and its predeclaration
(`PREDECLARED_retraining_envelope_20260804T0330Z.md`); `make_all.py --strict` raises on it; and F1
**refuses to draw the placeholder at all once the repeats report**, so the figure cannot go stale
unnoticed. F1's envelope is drawn as what it is meanwhile: two markers and the interval between them,
in grey, with `n = 1` on the panel. No shaded confidence region, because there is no interval.

**F8(b)'s 16/16 track has no per-step array and one cannot be manufactured.**
`~/e0_run/collapse_diag.log` — the source of record — probes before and after training, not on a
schedule. The hard-rank track and all four collapse-evidence quantities are drawn as before/after
paired markers labelled "endpoint values as recorded; per-step array not retained", with no line
between them that could be read as interpolation. The centred effective-rank track of the same
objective **is** per-step (`~/e0_run/d1_diag/diag_d.log`, steps 0/25/50/100/200/400) and is drawn as
a curve. The two are on one axis only because the withdrawal is precisely about mistaking one for the
other, and the panel says so.

**F8(a) row 1's statistic is left unnamed on purpose.** The diagnostic script's `eff-rank` column
cannot be assigned to R1, R2 or R3 from any committed source, so the row is labelled "diagnostic-script
centred effective rank (the diagnostic's own definition; NOT asserted equal to R1, R2 or R3 here)"
and shares no numeric axis with the R3 rows beneath it.

**F5's synthetic `(Σσ)²/Σσ² against (Σσ²)²/Σσ⁴` inset is not drawn here.** `P2_FIGURES.md` marks it
`NEEDS EXTRACTION` and asks for it as a separate computation under `v2/research/rebase/p2/`; the F5
docstring says it is absent and no stand-in is drawn for it.

### Binding constraints, discharged mechanically rather than by care

`p2fig.axis_label` refuses to build a rank axis without **both** a statistic and a block, so
constraint 1 cannot be violated by forgetting. No panel puts two rank statistics on one axis:
F4 separates (a)/(d)'s R1 from (c)'s R3 onto different axes; F3's R3 shares an axis with nothing.
F5(a) is drawn from `P2_RANK_VARIANTS.json` and never a printed table, labels the eigenvalue rows
`PR` / `PR_rownorm` and never R2/R3, and states on the panel that `PR` is identical cell for cell to
T1's participation-ratio row and is therefore not independent evidence. Every permutation null is its
own experiment's: F7(c) draws 0.145–0.147 and F8(c) draws D2's 0.140, and both panels say the two must
never share an axis. F6 draws **both** bootstrap estimators, weights the conservative one (thicker,
named first) and annotates seed 43's cancer interval grazing zero at +0.0006.
