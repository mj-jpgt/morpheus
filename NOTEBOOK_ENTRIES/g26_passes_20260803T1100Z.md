### Technical

The last defect was the gate's **update rule**, not its objective. `V2Trainer.train_epoch` clips the
gradient norm to 1.0; both G2.6 memorisation loops called `optimiser.step()` with no clipping at all.
So the gate was making a claim about an optimisation the real run never performs — and the difference
was not cosmetic. Seed 43, `programme_free`, unclipped:

| steps | 800 | 1600 | 2400 |
|---|---|---|---|
| unclipped | 0.1766 | 0.0477 | **11.7665** — diverged |
| clipped | **0.0121** | — | **0.0047** |

Unclipped, the run converged and then blew up, so the verdict depended on where the loop happened to
stop. Clipped, it descends monotonically and is 2.5× *further* below the threshold at 2400 steps than
at 800. Adding the clip to both arms' loops — matching `train_epoch` exactly — is the fix.

### The four defects, in the order they were found

1. **(pre-existing, fixed earlier)** the memory queue refreshed while the gate replayed one batch, so
   the negatives tracked the queries;
2. **(pre-existing, diagnosed earlier)** `feature_decorrelation` has total collapse as its global
   minimum and shipped without a variance floor;
3. **the uncentred contrastive objective**: ~81% of `z_biology`'s squared norm is one batch-common
   direction, so positives start *worse* than negatives (min margin −0.219), the loss starts at
   3.0762 — **above** chance — and erasing every distinction is a genuine descent direction ending on
   the permutation-symmetric saddle where the gradient is exactly zero. Effective rank 12.88 → 1.00
   in 50 steps. Fixed by removing each population's common direction before the similarity;
4. **the gate's own defects**: it ran three regularisers that each independently make a healthy model
   look dead (the variance floor alone costs a factor of 150 across a line drawn at 0.10), and it ran
   an unclipped optimiser that diverges. Both clones now exclude the batch-statistics regularisers —
   as `programme_only`'s clone always did — and both clip to 1.0 as training does.

Fix 2's proposed remedy — a variance floor beside the covariance penalty — was measured **not** to
work: a per-dimension floor is satisfied by the rank-1 family `z_i = m + a_i·u`, and weight 10.0 still
collapsed. Fix 3 is what actually addresses the collapse that fix 2 was aimed at.

**What was deliberately not changed:** the ≤ 0.10 threshold; the ≤ 0.02 consistency threshold; the
≥ 0.80 reduction requirement; the 800-step budget; the learning rates; the graded quantity
(`biology_contrastive` keeps its historical uncentred, queue-included definition, with the centred
value reported separately as `biology_contrastive_centred`); and every training weight, so both D1
arms still carry identical regularisation.

**Why relaxing the gate is not what happened.** A collapsed model scores ln 16 = 2.7726 on the graded
term. The contrastive criterion *is* the anti-collapse test, so removing regularisers from the
memorisation clone cannot let a collapsed model through — and the arm that passes does so with
16/16 retrieval and patient-to-patient cosine 0.0597 in the standalone harness.

### In plain terms

The final problem was that the health check was driving the model differently from the way the real
training drives it: real training reins in oversized updates, the check did not. On one of the three
seeds the check therefore trained the model to a good answer and then, given more steps, threw it
away — the score went 0.18, then 0.05, then 11.8. Making the check use the same rein as training
fixes it: the same seed now scores 0.012, and still 0.005 if you let it run three times longer.

All three seeds now pass, with the pass mark, the time limit and the learning rate all exactly where
they were. The model being trained is unchanged.

### Meaning for the claim

D1's entry condition is met on its own merits. The arm that had never existed on disk — a biology
head trained with no programme supervision — is now trainable, and the evidence that it is trainable
is a memorisation check that is stricter than it was before in two respects (it grades the uncentred
representation, and the consistency term it grades is now measured on the discriminative component
rather than on a shared offset).
