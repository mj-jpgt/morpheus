Flagging per the G2.6 degeneracy warning: **effective rank of `wsi_biology` is ~8.7 of 256
dimensions** in the Hallmark arm and ~7.1 in the partial PBS arm. `wsi_biology` rows are not
near-constant (the channel is real and residualises normally), but the representation is occupying
under 4% of its nominal dimensionality in *both* arms. This is not the `programme_free` collapse —
it is the `programme_only` path — but it is low enough that a D2 arm difference could be a rank
difference rather than a supervision-content difference. Effective rank is now recorded per arm per
seed as a predeclared counter-claim.

Note the `random_control` column is ~0.43–0.45, not ~0. That is expected: the top canonical
correlation between a 256-dim representation and a 90-dim target block is upward-biased by
dimensionality, so the *absolute* level is not interpretable without a null. A permutation null
(shuffling patient rows of the residualised target matrix, which preserves target count and
covariance and destroys only the correspondence) is being computed to calibrate it.

arms might be a difference in how much room each one uses rather than in what it knows.
